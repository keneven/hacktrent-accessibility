Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use the HackTrent website. That includes people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, screen magnification, voice control, or high contrast settings, and people who are colour blind.

Standard we aim for

This site targets the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. We have not had it independently audited, so we do not claim it is fully compliant.

What you can do on this site

  • Use every link, button and form field with a keyboard only, with a thick visible focus outline.
  • Skip straight to the main content with the first link on the page.
  • Zoom to 400% without content being cut off or scrolling sideways.
  • Use your system's dark mode, increased contrast, or Windows contrast theme.
  • Navigate by headings and landmarks with a screen reader.
  • Read the site in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, a typeface designed for low-vision readers.
  • Get form errors that say what went wrong and how to fix it, shown with an icon and text, not colour alone.
  • Turn off scrolling animation with your system's reduced motion setting.

What we have tested

These checks pass on both pages, in light and dark mode:

  • axe-core and the IBM Equal Access Checker: no WCAG 2.2 A or AA violations found.
  • Lighthouse accessibility: 100.
  • HTML validation: no errors.
  • An automated suite covering keyboard order, focus visibility, form errors, status messages, 320px reflow, 200% text, text spacing, reduced motion and forced colours.
  • Screenshots simulating deuteranopia, protanopia, tritanopia, full colour blindness and blurred vision, checked by eye.

Known gaps

  • The site has not yet been tested with a screen reader or a screen magnifier by a person, or by disabled users.
  • The Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and recap links are placeholders that do not go anywhere yet.
  • The search only searches this page.

Report a problem

If something on this site does not work for you, tell an organizer at the check-in desk or use the Contact section. We will fix it.
